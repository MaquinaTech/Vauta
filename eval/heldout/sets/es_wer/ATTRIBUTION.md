# Attribution of the Spanish reference texts

The `ref/es_vp_wer*.txt` files are transcripts of European Parliament plenary speech from the VoxPopuli corpus
(Wang et al., Meta AI, 2021; Hugging Face `facebook/voxpopuli`, revision `42f01879c780b4a2e90ec0b4f616c2ece526e4f1`,
Spanish test split). Licence: CC0-1.0. The utterance ids are listed in `info.json`.

The `ref/es_mls_wer*.txt` files are transcripts of LibriVox audiobook readings (public-domain books) from the
Multilingual LibriSpeech corpus (Pratap et al., Meta AI, 2020; Hugging Face `facebook/multilingual_librispeech`,
revision `2e83e61823b4c47dcbcb1980bb88601274127609`, Spanish dev and test splits). Licence: CC BY 4.0
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>). The texts are used unchanged except that utterances are concatenated.

The texts come from public sources and contain no private data. Some MLS texts use pre-reform Spanish spellings.
